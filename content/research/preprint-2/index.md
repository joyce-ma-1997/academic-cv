abstract: 
  How does firms’ technical knowledge translate into regulatory influence? I study more than 21,000 firm comments submitted to U.S. Environmental Protection Agency rulemakings from 2006 to 2024, linking comment text to patents, firm characteristics, lobbying, and EPA responses. Firms with greater technical expertise are more likely to influence final rules, particularly when that expertise is environmentally relevant. Beyond underlying expertise, technical disclosure provides a distinct channel of influence, especially when firms draw on their own technologies and operational experience. This informational channel is particularly important for startups, which more strongly convert their expertise into frontier technical disclosure and receive greater regulatory returns from doing so.
